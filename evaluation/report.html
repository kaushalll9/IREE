A chatbot that answers JEE/NEET students' questions by searching 43,116 r/JEENEETards posts, plus a crawler for official NTA notices. Every number is read from the evaluation outputs in evaluation/, except one figure: the before-fix official-answer score (18/20), recorded from the first evaluation run.

1 Web crawler: official NTA notices

The Reddit data ends in August 2022 and can't answer "when is the exam?". nta_crawler.py does a polite breadth-first crawl of the NTA sites. It collects every JEE/NEET notice, classifies each one by exam and type with ordered regular-expression rules, reads its upload date from the file name, and extracts the date lines from the PDF text. The chatbot routes official questions (an exam name plus an official topic such as admit card, result or last date) to the newest matching notice for the year asked.

1.2 Classification accuracy

Ground truth: all 107 notices hand-labelled by exam and type from their titles (evaluation/nta_labels.json, labelled by Claude). The first version of the classifier was measured against these labels. Its errors fell into three patterns: "NTA Scores" declarations not counted as results, "Conduct of…/Rescheduling" notices not counted as dates, and fee refunds counted as applications. Those rules were then fixed. The before-fix accuracy is the honest number; the after-fix accuracy is optimistic because the fixes were made using the same labels.

1.3 Extracting dates from PDFs

NTA's important-dates tables have no ruling lines. pypdf's text extraction split them into roughly one word per line, so the row "Date of Examination 20, 21, 22, 23, 24 and 28 January 2027" was never extracted and the bot answered a 2027 question with a 2025 notice. Switching to pdfplumber, which keeps each row on one line, fixed this. Both libraries were run on the same 20 PDFs:

10 of the 20 PDFs are scanned images with no text layer, so neither library gets anything from them; OCR would be the fix.

1.4 Publication dates

107 of 107 notices get an exact upload date from the NTA file name (/uploads/2026/04/20260409….pdf → 2026-04-09). Only 4 PDFs print a parseable "Dated:" line to check against: 50% match exactly; the others were uploaded 17, 16 days after the date printed on them (Syllabus for NEET (UG)-2026 Examination, Syllabus for NEET (UG) 2025 Examination ). So the stored date is the upload date, which is what "latest notice" needs.

2 Domain dictionary: TF-IDF + embeddings

Students write "pw", "hcv" and "hall ticket"; posts say "physics wallah", "hc verma" and "admit card". The dictionary adds such terms to the query at reduced weight (0.5 × similarity; the original words keep weight 1.0). Three ways of building it were compared, all with the same 92 seed terms in 8 categories and the same expansion rules (only exam, coaching, resource, admission and student terms expand; sibling seeds and digit tokens are never added; at most 3 terms per seed).

VariantHow related terms are found
TF-IDF only (co-occurrence)Terms whose TF-IDF document vectors are most similar to the seed's (they occur in the same posts). Statistics only, no embeddings.
Embeddings only (Word2Vec)Nearest neighbours of the seed in a Word2Vec model trained on the posts (skip-gram, 150-d, window 5), cosine ≥ 0.55.
Hybrid (Word2Vec + TF-IDF)Word2Vec neighbours, kept only if TF-IDF ranks them among the 3,000 most salient terms in the corpus. Embeddings decide what means the same; TF-IDF decides what matters.

Pipeline: tokenise → detect phrases (gensim Phrases: "physics wallah" → physics_wallah) → TF-IDF over 43,116 posts (11,559 terms in ≥5 posts) and Word2Vec (12,076-word vocabulary) → dictionary of 815 terms (92 seeds + 723 learned).

2.1 Dictionary accuracy: are the added terms right?

Every (seed → added term) pair that a variant can put into a query was graded: same meaning (synonym, abbreviation, spelling variant), related, or unrelated, for 183 unique pairs in total (evaluation/dictionary_pairs.json, graded by Claude).

VariantDictionary termsExpansion pairsSame meaningRelated or betterUnrelated (drift)
TF-IDF only (co-occurrence)262791%38%62%
Embeddings only (Word2Vec)81511034%80%20%
Hybrid (Word2Vec + TF-IDF)8155857%91%9%

The hybrid is the most accurate dictionary. The TF-IDF filter removes rare neighbours (hbcse, aptitude test, official answer, sankalp), which were the main source of drift, and keeps salient ones (physics wallah, admit card, cbse). TF-IDF on its own finds words that appear alongside a seed (josaa → documents, round), not words that mean the same, so only 1% of its additions are synonyms.

Reading the results honestly. On search quality the four variants are within noise of each other and of no expansion: the differences are about ±0.01 NDCG@10, and no p-value comes near 0.05 (paired randomization test, 10,000 permutations). With BM25 the hybrid is best (0.523) and hurts the fewest queries (6 vs 9 for embeddings only). In the deployed system, TF-IDF only scores highest on the test split (0.532) despite its poor dictionary, because related words act as topical context. On the tuning split, however, embeddings only did best there. The hybrid's clear win is dictionary accuracy (fewer wrong expansions shown to users as "Also searched: …"), not ranking.

4 Method and caveats

  • Who labelled the data. Relevance judgments: 1,863 by Claude (an LLM, graded against each query's stated intent) and 143 from the original project. The notice labels, the dictionary-pair grades and the official-question expectations were also made by Claude. All are saved in evaluation/ and should be spot-checked by a person (python evaluate.py annotate --review).
  • Tuning vs testing. Every tuned setting (RRF weight, TF-IDF threshold, salience cut-off) was chosen on the 24 tuning queries; all reported search numbers are on the 56 test queries.
  • Unjudged documents are skipped (condensed rankings), not counted as irrelevant; every system's top 10 is 99–100% judged.
  • Small differences are noise. With 56 test queries, differences under ~0.03 NDCG@10 are not significant.
  • Crawler accuracy after fixes is optimistic, because the fixes were made using the same labels and questions. The before-fix numbers (81.3% type accuracy, 90% official answers) are the unbiased ones.
  • Data age. The Reddit data ends in August 2022; official dates come from the live NTA crawl.
