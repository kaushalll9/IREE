💡 Theoretical & Empirical Insights

1. Title Weighting (BM25F) Cuts Both Ways

Reddit titles often state the question ("DC Pandey vs HC Verma"), so weighting the title 2.5× improves the graded ordering of results (NDCG@10). But it also promotes bodiless meme posts whose title happens to match ("IIT BOMBAY CSE pakka ab 🗿"), which costs precision at rank 1 against plain BM25.

2. Role of the Engagement Boosting Factor

A subdued logarithmic upvote boost (score × (1 + 0.05·log₁₀(1 + upvotes))) breaks ties toward community-endorsed threads and recovers most of BM25F's MAP loss. Boosting by comment count instead was tested and lowered MAP, so it is not used.

3. Domain Expansion and Honest Judgments

Embedding-based query expansion adds synonyms and slang (hall ticket → admit card, hcv → hc verma). It is neutral on this benchmark, whose queries already match their posts word for word, and helps on vocabulary-mismatch queries. Relevance judgments come from pooling every configuration's top 10; with 10 queries, small differences are within noise.
